    All counties

    Change = 2022 rate minus 2018 rate. Gap = county rate minus statewide rate for the same age group and election. Both in percentage points (pts).

    Methodology

    • Turnout rate = ballots cast ÷ registered voters (active + inactive) for each county, age group, and election.
    • Age groups combine the Secretary of State's 5-year brackets: 25-29 + 30-34 → 25-34, and so on; 65 and older → 65+. Age comes from the voter file's birth year.
    • 2018: SoS "Voter Turnout by Demographics" report (Nov 6, 2018 general). 2022: SoS Election Data Hub, Nov 8, 2022 general (voter base as of the Oct 11, 2022 registration deadline). 6,115 ballots in 2022 (0.15%) had no matching age record and are excluded from age-group figures.
    • Registered counts include inactive voters, so rates are lower than "% of active voters" figures some SoS dashboards show.

    Data sources: Georgia Secretary of State — Voter Turnout by Demographics (2018); Election Data Hub, Turnout & Demographics (2022). County boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary files (via us-atlas).
